Installation guide

Step-by-step setup for every platform.

Windows installation

Downloadable .exe installer for Windows 11.

  1. Download the installer from the Assets section.
  2. Temporarily turn off Windows SmartScreen if it blocks the installer.
  3. Open the .exe.
  4. Follow the installation instructions.
  5. Turn Windows SmartScreen back on immediately after installation.
  6. Launch Sxarf AI.
  7. On first launch, Sxarf AI checks available disk space and prepares the required environment and AI models.
  8. When setup finishes, start chatting!

macOS installation

Downloadable .dmg installer with Apple MLX optimizations.

  1. Download the .dmg from the Assets section.
  2. Open it and drag Sxarf AI into your Applications folder.
  3. Launch Sxarf AI and wait for setup to finish.
  4. If prompted, enter your Mac password to grant Sxarf AI access to its local data.

iPhone installation

Downloadable .ipa installer. Choose a signing method below.

Method 1: KSign or ESign

  1. Download the .ipa from the Assets section.
  2. Install KSign or ESign.
  3. Import the Sxarf AI .ipa file.
  4. Sign and install the application using a compatible signing method.
  5. Open Sxarf AI and wait for setup to finish.
Disclaimer: KSign and ESign are convenient, but their availability and signing services may change or become unavailable. If neither works, use Sideloadly as an alternative. Tools such as iLoader or SideStore can also work, subject to compatible signing and installation requirements.

Method 2: Sideloadly

  1. Get a laptop and a USB cable to connect your iPhone.
  2. Download Sideloadly from sideloadly.io (macOS and Windows).
  3. Install and open Sideloadly.
  4. Connect your iPhone to your computer.
  5. Enter your Apple ID when prompted for signing.
  6. Click the IPA file selection button and locate the downloaded Sxarf AI .ipa.
  7. Click Start.
  8. Wait until Sideloadly reports Done.
  9. If required, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management on your iPhone and trust the developer profile.
  10. Open Sxarf AI and wait for setup to finish.

Android installation

Downloadable .apk installer. ARM64 devices only; 32-bit systems are not supported.

  1. Download the .apk from the Assets section.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If prompted, allow your browser or file manager to install apps from this source.
  3. Complete the installation.
  4. Open Sxarf AI and wait for setup to finish.
  5. Start chatting!
